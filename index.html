About Swamini Aari Work

Traditional Craftsmanship Meets Modern Design

Swamini Aari Work is a boutique embroidery studio specializing in the traditional Aari needlework technique, passed down through generations. Each piece is meticulously handcrafted with attention to detail and quality.

Our founder, Sayali Dipak Jadhav, brings years of expertise and passion to every creation, blending traditional techniques with contemporary designs to create unique, wearable art.
